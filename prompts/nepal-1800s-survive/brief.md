# Video Brief: "Could You Survive 1810s Nepal?"

Master brief for the `directing-stickman-videos` skill. It contains every setup decision, the frozen character and voice blocks, a clip-by-clip story map and the facts that need checking. The skill turns it into a Phase A director's proposal (full narration and storyboard) and, once that is approved, 18 standalone Gemini Omni Flash prompts.

## 2. Core message

> Life in a hill village 200 years ago wasn't "simple and peaceful". It was hard work from before dawn to after dark, with no doctor, no clock and no safety net except other people. They lacked almost everything we have. But they had something many of us have lost.

**Hook (draft):** *"You wake before dawn. No light switch. No clock. The nearest doctor? There isn't one. It's 1810, in the hills of Nepal. Would you last a week?"*

**Closing question (draft):** *"They had no medicine, but they had each other. We have medicine, and we scroll alone. So tell me: what did we lose, and what did we gain?"*

## 5. Look, palette and sound

**Environment:** Terraced hillsides stepping down into a misty valley, distant snow-capped Himalayan peaks, two-story stone-and-mud farmhouse with a thatched roof and small wooden window, packed-earth courtyard, pine and rhododendron forest, narrow stone footpaths. Full-color 2D cartoon with clear contours, cinematic framing and soft natural light. Not sketchbook, not photoreal, not 3D.

**Palette mood (plain words, no color codes):**
- cool dawn blue-gray: waking, uncertainty (Clips 1–2)
- fresh terrace green and millet gold: work and harvest
- heavy slate gray and rain-dark brown: monsoon, illness, danger (Clips 7–8)
- warm firelight amber: home, healing, community (Clips 9, 13, 16, 18)
- snow-peak white and sky blue: the trail, reflection (Clips 11–12)
- the rust-red sash is the protagonist's signature color

**Music (one motif for the whole video):** A gentle folk theme on sarangi (Nepali bowed fiddle) with soft bansuri flute and a light madal hand-drum pulse. Same tempo, key and instruments throughout, starting in Clip 1. It thins out in Clips 7–8, swells warmly in Clips 14–16 and resolves softly in Clip 18.

**SFX (environmental, never on the voice):** crackling embers, a rooster at dawn, trickling spring water, an ox bell, stone grinding stone, forest birds, monsoon downpour, distant thunder, the healer's drum, wind on the high trail, a night-fire crackle, a creaking bamboo swing.

**Text on screen:** none in generation. Optional post-production overlays are listed in §8.

## 7. Facts used (please check)

The narration uses only these broad claims. Confirm or cut any you're unsure of before Phase A:

1. Nepal was unified under the Gorkha kingdom in the late 1700s. The **Anglo-Nepal War** with the British East India Company was fought **1814–1816** and ended with the Treaty of Sugauli.
2. Hill farmers worked **terraced fields** growing crops such as **rice and millet** (maize is also often cited for this period).
3. Houses were commonly **stone-and-mud** with **thatched** roofs.
4. Grain was ground on a **hand-turned stone mill (jaanto)**.
5. Loads were carried in a **cone-shaped woven basket (doko) with a forehead strap (namlo)**.
6. **Traditional healers (dhami/jhankri)** used drums and ritual, and there was no modern medicine in rural hills.
7. Hill people traded **grain for salt** brought from the Tibetan side.
8. The monsoon brought **landslides** that could damage terraces.
9. **Dashain** (with bamboo swings, called *ping*) and **Tihar** (with oil lamps) were autumn festivals after the harvest.
10. The **khukuri** was the everyday curved knife of the hills.

There are no statistics, dates beyond #1, or quotations.

## 9. Global constraints for every clip prompt

- Repeat each appearing character's **full frozen design block** verbatim. Never write "same character" as a shortcut.
- Repeat the **full frozen voice block** verbatim in all 18 clips.
- Keep each prompt in four scopes: static design → movable props → first frame and timed transfers → final state.
- Movable props get their own definitions and owners: water pot, wooden plow and yoke, stone hand-mill, woven basket with forehead strap, sickle, khukuri, clay oil lamps, healer's drum, covered bowl.
- No visible words, letters, numbers or captions in the scene. Icons only.
- No color codes. Ordinary color words only.
- No extra people. Exactly two arms and two legs per character.
- No abstract liquid or shape morphing. Motion comes from concrete actions.
- 16:9 staging: left, center and right action; lateral tracking; travel direction stays mostly **left to right** across the journey.
- Keep the narrator voice close and dry; location ambience goes in SFX.
